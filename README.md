# InterviewAce AI 🎯

### AI-Powered Interview Preparation Portal

InterviewAce AI is an intelligent interview preparation platform designed to help students and job seekers improve their interview skills and build confidence before attending real-world interviews.

The platform provides AI-powered mock interviews, technical question practice, coding challenges, HR preparation, behavioral interview simulations, and aptitude tests. Students can attend mock interviews using their webcam and microphone, answer questions through voice or text, and receive personalized performance evaluations.

## 🚀 Features

- **User Authentication:** Secure login and registration.
- **Student Dashboard:** Track interview performance and learning progress.
- **AI Mock Interviews:** Practice interviews based on job role, category, and difficulty.
- **Webcam Integration:** Attend realistic online mock interviews using a camera and microphone.
- **Voice and Text Answers:** Answer questions using speech-to-text or typing.
- **Technical Preparation:** Practice Python, Java, C, C++, DBMS, DSA, SQL, AI, and ML.
- **Coding Sandbox:** Solve programming challenges using different programming languages.
- **HR Preparation:** Practice common HR interview questions.
- **Behavioral Interviews:** Practice scenario-based interview questions.
- **Aptitude Tests:** Improve quantitative, logical, verbal, and analytical skills.
- **AI Evaluation:** Evaluate actual submitted answers and generate performance feedback.
- **Interview History:** Track previous interview sessions and scores.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Sign in to the platform.
2. Access the personalized student dashboard.
3. Select an interview preparation module.
4. Choose the job role, subject, difficulty, and number of questions.
5. Start practicing through text, voice, or webcam-enabled mock interviews.
6. Answer or skip questions.
7. Submit completed answers.
8. Receive an AI-generated performance report.
9. Track progress through interview history.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of InterviewAce AI is to provide students with an accessible and interactive interview preparation environment that simulates real interview experiences and helps them identify their strengths and areas for improvement.
